Pin Clarigo for Quick Access

You’ll want to turn Clarigo on and off when you switch between study mode and breaks. Keep it one click away:

  1. 1 Click the puzzle piece icon in your Chrome toolbar (top-right).
  2. 2 Find Clarigo in the list and click the pin icon next to it.
  3. 3 Clarigo’s icon will stay visible so you can open the popup anytime.

How the Filter Works

Clarigo’s AI evaluates videos in real time. It’s smart, but sometimes an educational video might get caught in the filter, or a distraction might slip through.

Quick Tips
