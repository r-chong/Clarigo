Clarigo Settings

Study-mode YouTube, tuned locally.

Clarigo scores titles and channel names in the browser, then hides sponsored noise, Shorts, and videos that do not clear your educational threshold.

Enable filter

Turn Clarigo on or off without losing your saved mode.

Filtering style

Aggressive mode is the default because this extension is meant to behave like deliberate study armor, not a gentle recommender tweak.

Debug logging

Log scores, thresholds, and suppression reasons in the page console.

What Clarigo hides before the model

  • Sponsored cards and promoted YouTube units
  • Shorts shelves and other noisy recommendation containers
  • Videos that fail the current educational threshold
